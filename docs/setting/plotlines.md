# Plotlines

Beyond the leaderboard and regular gameplay, there are some longer-arc plotlines that the player can interact with. These are optional and not always aligned towards profitable actions.

## Jerusalem Ridge

Player can find the translation solution back to Old Earth, and to Mars. One settlement on Mars still shows a signature: Jerusalem Ridge. It doesn't respond to hails.

## Asciidians

Research by a corporation provokes an Ascidians to sortey into realspace.

## SolCoin Origin wallet

The oldest SolCoin wallet is only labelled "origin" and is dated to 2111. It holds an unknown amount of funds, likely very large.

## Missing pilot

A celebrity pilot disappears.

## GRC vs Creus

These two corporations wage a war in the shadows that results in one being destroyed.

## Lost route

A high-N route between two major destinations has stopped working for some reason.
