# Planets and sectors

## Proxima

 - **Classification:** E1  
 - **System:** Alpha Centauri A  
 - **Gravity:** .98 G  
 - **Population:** 60 billion  
 - **Ocean coverage:** 71%  
 - **Climate:** Temperate

Proxima was the first E-class planet discovered and colonised by humans, and is today the galactic capital. It is a lush, densely populated world with many large cities.

Proxima's skies are busy: Alpha Centauri B appears bright enough to be visible even during the day, and the red dwarf Alpha Centauri C dominates the night starscape. For this, and its ceaseless commercial activity, Proxima is often called "the planet that never sleeps". 

The Proxima Peaceforce is responsible for maintaining order in the shared commercial environment.

### Solar neighbourhood

Rather unusually, Proxima is the only planet orbiting Alpha Centauri A, a G-type star. Beyond its orbit lies a broad asteroid belt, heavily exploited for mining and industry but still containing remote and hazardous regions. 

Alpha Centauri B, the system's K-type companion star, has its own small planetary system of several uninhabitable worlds and industrial installations. Far beyond both stellar systems is a sparse circumbinary region of icy bodies and minor planets.

 - Centauri A Beltworks: a central habitat for mining operations in the Alpha Centauri A asteroid belt
 - ACB1: hot rocky inner planet of Alpha Centauri B, tidally locked, magma and volcanoes
 - ACB2: also called "Scar", this is a rocky inner planet of Alpha Centauri B, no atmosphere, mining operations are present on the surface with orbital support
 - ACB3: a small ice covered planet near Alpha Centauri B's orbital boundary
 - Terminus: a large frozen planet on a distant circum-binary orbit

### Field Conditions

 - Solar: Stable Alpha Centauri A with fluctuating influence from the nearby Alpha Centauri B
 - Magnetic: nominal magnetic dipole

## Tycho

 - **Classification:** E3  
 - **System:** Regulus A
 - **Gravity:** 1.07 G
 - **Population:** 3.5 billion  
 - **Ocean coverage:** 62%  
 - **Climate:** Arid

Although on paper Tycho seems a tame world, the reality is quite different. Tycho is an E-class planet where none should be: amongst a family of fifteen gas giants orbiting a fierce blue star. It suffers from wildly fluctuating magnetic poles, and severe earth tremors and volcanoes. Still it is resource rich and industrialised.

Tycho's skies are famously chaotic. The blazing blue oval Regulus and its often-visible white companion dominate the day. They are often eclipsed by one of the inner giants, Denarius in particular often being visible in the dayside sky. At night the two inner gas giants are prominently visible and their distant relatives march across the skies on various trajectories. The distant stellar companions Regulus BC are the brightest star in the night sky.

### Planetary Orbit

### Solar neighbourhood

Regulus A is itself a close binary: a rapidly rotating bright blue star with a faint white dwarf companion (called The Widow by the earliest Tycho settlers) at only 0.35 AU.

Two volatile gas giants keep tight orbits around the pair, then a good deal further out lies Tycho in a narrow habitable zone. Beyond Tycho is an asteroid belt, followed by a gaggle of increasingly distant gas giants, some in very eccentric orbits, and their numerous moons.

 - Regulus Belt Station: habitat central to mining the asteroid belt beyond Tycho
 - Vulcan: The innermost gas giant orbiting Regulus, a Neptune-sized hot ball of toxic storms. A heavily shielded scientific observation habitat is in orbit.
 - Denarius II: The second moon of the second gas giant (called Denarius because it's visible as a coin-sized silver disk in Tycho's skies). This is a rocky moon with surface and orbital mining infrastructure.
 - Typhon XVI: The 16th moon of the gas giant Typhon, a huge turbulent world with a spectacular ring system. The moon is rocky and unstable, with surface and orbital mining infrastructure.
 - others TBD

### Field conditions

 - Solar: Regulus very radiant and windy
 - Magnetic: large, restless magnetic dipole with high fluctuation

## La Bella Vista

 - **Classification:** E4  
 - **System:** Delta Pavonis
 - **Gravity:** 0.95 G  
 - **Population:** 15 billion  
 - **Ocean coverage:** 92%  
 - **Climate:** Warm  

La Bella Vista, often called Bela, is a water world with only one continental land mass, orbiting a quiet G-type star. It is the most popular holiday resort in the galaxy. All year round the rich and idle flock to the idyllic atolls of Bela, or visit its mega-casinos and orbital hotels.

 - City Malls: Oberon CM, Santa Margarita, Coral Bay CM, Paradise CM, Marina CM, Bellevue CM, Laguna Arc, Côte d'Azur, Marbella CM, Capri CM, Valencia Arc, Zaytun CM, Sapphire CM, Nimitz Stack
 - Other: Watershed Stadium near Oberon is a famous outdoor cricket stadium

### Planetary Orbit

 - La Bella Vista Habitat
 - others TBD

### Solar neighbourhood

Delta Pavonis has a busy orbit of ten planets (Bela being the fourth of them), some of which are easily reachable via translation beacons. The tourism industry on the main E-class world spills out into these other accessible places in the system.

Planets and moons:
 - Aurelia: the third planet (0.55 AU); a dry rocky world with a marginal atmosphere. Mining and research infrastructure, as well as tourist locations for low-G hab-suit mountaineering, rock climbing and base jumping.
 - Boreas: the fifth planet (1.6 AU); an icy super-E-type with a marginal atmosphere. Mineral-rich, but its high gravity makes it unpopular with tourists.
 - Calypso VI: sixth moon of Calypso, the sixth planet (3.2 AU) and the first gas giant in the system. The moon-orbiting resort has famous views of the parent's spectacular ring system.
 - Hesper: the eighth planet (11 AU); an ice giant on an eccentric orbit. It has an unusually strong magnetic field.

Others:
 - Kestrel: not a planet, but a long-period comet with a very large icy nucleus. It's orbited by a resort habitat. The comet's eccentric orbit leads to seasonal activities built around the comet's changing activity as it approaches Delta Pavonis.

### Field conditions

 - Solar influence: Delta Pavonis is warm with elevated radiance and moderate wind
 - Magnetic: slightly weaker dipole than a capital world

## Irasia

 - **Classification:** E5  
 - **System:** 61 Virginis
 - **Gravity:** .99 G
 - **Population:** 46 billion  
 - **Ocean coverage:** 76%  
 - **Climate:** Temperate

Irasia is a prosperous and heavily industrialised world, with enormous coastal cities and extensive manufacturing districts. Its temperate climate and abundant oceans support a large population, while its position on major trade routes has made it one of the galaxy’s principal centres of commerce.

### Planet Surface

 - City Malls: La Palma, Fairhaven CM, Albion CM, New Singapore, Grand Exchange CM, Commerce CM, Samarkand Stack, Isfahan CM, Amber CM, Ring of Fire CM, Ormuz Arc, Zaytun Arc, Pearl City Stack, New Malacca, Jade CM, New Zanzibar Arc, Kamchatka Stack, Imperial Arc, New Westminster, La Salle CM, St Moritz CM, Syracuse CM

### Planetary Orbit

 - Irasia Habitat
 - Orion Aerospace HQ
 - others TBD

### Solar neighbourhood

Irasia orbits the stable and steady 61 Virginis at 0.99 AU, one of nine planets in the system. Unusually for an E class world Irasia has four small moons in varying orbits, the outermost quite inclined.

Planets and moons:
 - Alecto (planet 1): a large rocky planet nearest 61 Virginis, tidally locked at 0.05 AU. Scorched and airless, the darkside hosts automated mining operations.
 - New Ceres (planet 6): a dense super-E, the sixth planet out beyond Irasia at 1.5 AU. It's thick, toxic atmosphere of greenhouse gasses is unbreathable but still useful for extensive agriculture operations.
 - Varda III (moon 3 of planet 7): seventh planet and the largest gas giant in the system, Varda has a small ring system and a distinctive silvery atmosphere. The third moon is site of resource extraction from the subsurface ocean beneath it's icy surface.
 - Cressida: the ninth and last planet, on an unusually inclined orbit. Cressida is a small world, cold but with active cryovulcanism. A lonely research station orbits it.

 Other
 - Virginis Asteroid Station: the asteroid belt beyond New Ceres is rich in exotic minerals, crystals and chemicals
 - Varda Asteroid Station: beyond Varda lies another, sparser asteroid belt, also a site of resource extraction

### Field conditions

 - Solar influence: 61 Virginis near-baseline sunlike radiance and wind
 - Magnetic: calm dipole

### Solar neighbourhood

Tokirev is one of just five planets orbiting the young G class star. 

Planets and moons:

## Fennet

 - **Classification:** E1  
 - **System:** 18 Scorpii
 - **Gravity:** .95 G
 - **Population:** 29 billion  
 - **Ocean coverage:** 78%  
 - **Average temperature:** 20°C  

Fennet is a populous, temperate world that has grown from a once-isolated frontier settlement into an important regional centre. Its calm oceans and mild climate support large agricultural and urban areas, while its position on the rim gives it a distinctive mixture of established industry and newer development.

### Planet Surface

City Malls: New Belfast, Pioneer CM, Homestead CM, Greenbelt CM, Ash Stack, New Dunedin, Larkspur CM, Settlers CM, Stirling CM, Penang CM, New Eden Arc, Fairmead Arc, Bitters Stack, Kirkland CM, New Benoni, Table CM, Busan CM, Valhalla CM

### Planetary orbit

 - Fennet Habitat
 - others TBD

### Solar neighbourhood

At 0.98 AU, Fennet is the inner planet of the 18 Scorpii system. Four other planets and some eccentric dwarfs complete the major orbital bodies, but an unusually dense comet cloud on a profusion of orbits fills the outer system. The skies of Fennet are famous for always showcasing at a variety passing cometary bodies.

Planets and moons:
 - Junker's World (2.1 AU, 2nd planet): The only other non-gas giant in the system, Junker's World is a rocky wasteland with a dense Nitrogen atmosphere. Surface mineral extraction takes place, notably including the renowned Junkers Emeralds.

Other:
 - Swan: a short period high-albedo comet so named for it's opposed polar outgassing. Rare mineral extraction and research is supported by a small orbital.
 - The Freediver: a highly eccentric cometary body that approaches very close to 18 Scorpii. Chemical and energy extraction from it's core is supported by largely automated infrastructure - and paused during perihelion.
 - Nostromo: an icy-covered comet with an unusual radioactive core. Research station.
 - Eternum: a very long period icy comet that has only visited Fennet once during human habitation there. A small resort habitat supports the dedicated adventure tourists who want to tick this one off.

### Field neighborhood

 - Solar influence: 18 Scorpii with quiet radiance and wind
 - Magnetic: standard dipole.

## Fortuna

 - **Classification:** E2
 - **System:** Tau Ceti
 - **Gravity:** 1.01 G
 - **Population:** 34 billion
 - **Ocean coverage:** 68%
 - **Climate:** Temperate

Fortuna is a wealthy and densely populated world whose prosperity was built on finance, trade and speculation. Its cities are filled with banks, exchanges, corporate headquarters and luxury developments, while enormous agricultural regions provide food for its population. The planet is known for its comfortable standard of living and its unusually active commercial culture.

### Planet Surface

### Planetary Orbit

 - Fortuna Habitat
 - GRC Inc HQ
 - The Octagon HQ
 - others TBD

### Solar neighbourhood

Fortuna is Tau Ceti' second planet, orbiting 0.7 AU from it's dim parent star. The system of six planets is surrounded by a notable debris disc spanning the orbits from 11 to 50 AU. It's unclear why there is an unusually large surviving reservoir of planetesimals and debris.

Planets and moons:
 - Janus (1.5 AU, 3rd planet): An frozen rocky world lacking a magnetic field, and with the barest traces of atmosphere. Resource extraction on Janus is possible but often not very profitable due to the harsh conditions, high gravity and thick ice sheets.
 - Sapphire XVI (6.1 AU, 16th moon of the 5th planet): This moon surface is heaped with frozen methane and other chemical resources. It's gas giant parent is so called for it's unusual blueish bands.
 - Vesuvius V: (8 AU, 5th moon of 6th planet): Vesuvius is the systems signature gas giant, notably for it's extremely colourful and turbulent atmosphere. The 5th moon is rich in valuable minerals.

Other:
 - TC Inner Disc Station: Tau Ceti's debris disk is extensively exploited for resource extraction. The inner belt station is the hub for activities in the very dense 11-19 AU band.
 - TC Outer Disc Station: This is the hub for resource extraction in the less dense outer debris disc.
 - Terminary Research Station: An isolated research station just beyond the debris disc.

### Field conditions

 - Solar influence: Quiet Tau Ceti - lower radiant and particle wind
 - Magnetic: stable dipole

## New Carthage

 - **Classification:** E4
 - **System:** 61 Cygni A
 - **Gravity:** 0.98 G
 - **Population:** 27 billion
 - **Ocean coverage:** 84%
 - **Climate:** Warm

New Carthage is a largely oceanic world whose major population centres developed around a network of artificial islands and enormous coastal cities. Originally settled as a trading colony, it became a major shipping and financial hub. Much of the planet's culture remains shaped by commerce, with old mercantile institutions sitting alongside vast modern corporate developments.

### Planetary orbit

 - New Carthage Habitat
 - Crown & Anchor HQ

### Solar neighbourhood

New Carthage is the fourth of five planets orbitting 61 Cygni A. It's single moon, Byrsa, is large and has a great deal of tidal influence. Both Byrsa and the solar partner, orange 61 Cygni B, are usually brightly visible in the night skies.

Planets and moons:
 - Byrsa (only moon of 4th planet): New Carthage's own large moon is orbited by significant orbital infrastructure.
 - Tanit XX (8 AU, 20th moon of the 5th planet): 61 Cygni A's only gas giant, Tanit, is a extremely large and displays spectacular white and red bands. The 20th moon has active warm subsurface oceans and is a useful site for both mining and energy generation.
 - Nightjar: (3.5 AU, 3rd planet of 61 Cygni B): The partner star has three planets, the last of which is a small, dark rocky world heavily developed for mining.

Other:
 - Depot 61C: a populated hub in the 61 Cygni A asteroid belt between the 3rd and 4th planets.

### Field conditions

 - Solar influence: Dim 61 Cygni, low radiant and wind
 - Magnetic: modest dipole
