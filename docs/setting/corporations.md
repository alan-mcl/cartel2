# Corporations

## Galactic Outcomes(GO)

Galactic Outcomes has its roots in the turbulent African politics of the late twentieth and early twenty-first century. The company began by supplying local mercenaries to the highest bidder, a lucrative market at the time. Today they are by far the largest supplier of all forms of hired muscle from bouncers and bodyguards to soldiers and assassins. They include a profitable subdivision that manufactures all forms of small arms and ammunition for both the personal and corporate market, and another that specializes in non-combat personnel like scientists, engineers and accountants.

## Karaquazen Inc (KRQ)

Karaquazen is a corporation respected for its monopoly as much as it is feared for its methods. It runs a galactic chain of mega-casinos and gambling houses, and operates 95% of all the planetary lotteries. Although it is not widely known, Karaquazen also has a controlling interest in nearly all medical aid and insurance providers.

## Himalaya Triad (HT)

The corporation traces its origins to Tibetan triads that emerged in the late twenty-first century. It has extensive interests in pharmaceuticals, personal services, security, finance and specialist contracting, and has a reputation for decisive and occasionally ruthless business practices.

## MicroDonald Franchise (MDF)

Born of the merger between several Americano-descended companies, MicroDonald specialises in low cost, high volume consumer goods and services of all kinds. Behind its high-powered advertising campaign and cute mascot, MDF is a cunning and ruthless monopoly that has annihilated all competition. It is possible, and increasingly common, for a person to use exclusively MDF products from the moment he is delivered in a city-mall hospital to the day he is buried in an MDF coffin.

## ParaRamcoVidia (PRV)

ParaRamcoVidia is a vast conglomerate and a dominant force in media, entertainment, energy, computing and communications, with extensive interests across manufacturing, infrastructure, consumer technology and research. Its media division controls major broadcast networks, studios and entertainment platforms, while its energy and technology divisions provide essential infrastructure throughout human space.

## GRC Inc (GRC)

GRC is the youngest of the large corporations, and it is in direct competition with the older and larger Creus Corp. Both corporations deal in the investment banking and speculation market. GRC sprang into being a mere two generations ago when an uncharacteristic slip by Creus left the market wide open for a competitor.

## Creus Corporation (CRC)

Creus is an old and respected investment bank and one of the richest corporations out there, at least in terms of declared assets. Their banking operations are under threat from the upstart GRC, but they still hold the monopoly when it comes to auction houses, micro loans and property markets.

## Holt-Winters Corp (HW)

Holt-Winters is a young, ambitious conglomerate with a reputation for innovation and aggressive expansion. It is a major player in energy, aerospace, electronics and advanced materials, with particularly strong positions in compact power systems and shield technology. Holt-Winters also operates extensive consumer and industrial businesses, and its light spacecraft are popular with independent operators.

## Tukey Enterprises (TE)

Tukey Enterprises is a diversified commercial conglomerate with interests across manufacturing, logistics, technology and consumer goods. It has built its reputation by entering overlooked markets and supplying products that larger corporations consider too small or uninteresting to pursue. This opportunistic strategy has made Tukey a surprisingly influential supplier across human space, with a broad and constantly changing portfolio.

## SnedeCorp (SNE)

Software is the game of SnedeCorp. It has over time amalgamated thousands of different software companies to gain the monopoly in the market. They manufacture all kinds of software from games to commercial software to the complex systems found on modern space ships. SnedeCorp has a reputation as a paranoid and secretive company.

## Oklahoma Combine (OC)

The Oklahoma Combine began as a consortium of Americano agricultural, energy and engineering firms. It is a major supplier of industrial machinery, spacecraft and infrastructure. The Combine retains a reputation for practicality, reliability and formidable logistical capacity.

## Sakuraya Shinise (SS)

Sakuraya Shinise claims to be humanity’s oldest continuously operating business, originating as a small Japanese family tea shop in Nippon, Old Earth. Today it is a vast and highly diversified conglomerate with interests spanning manufacturing, finance, food, hospitality, technology and transportation. The founding family remains closely involved, and Sakuraya’s traditional culture is famously conservative despite its enormous scale.

## Cult of Apex (APEX)

The Cult traces its origins to a late twenty-first-century hacker and religious radical who died after hijacking a NASA launch and sending his writings and personal effects into deep space as a time capsule. Recovered centuries later, the capsule inspired a powerful new faith whose institutions grew into a vast corporate empire.

## Carthage Mercantile (CM)

Carthage Mercantile traces its lineage to Mediterranean trading houses that survived through centuries of political upheaval by continually reinventing themselves. Today it is a sprawling conglomerate spanning shipping, finance, commodities, agriculture, luxury goods and communications. Its executives maintain an almost obsessive interest in trade routes and commercial intelligence.

## Pacific Triad (PT)

Descended from the Sino-Americano-Hanguk criminal fraternity. Today the Triad's interests span finance, shipping, entertainment, security, pharmaceuticals and consumer goods. Its enormous commercial network is built on generations of family relationships and personal connections, and it remains one of the galaxy’s most influential corporations, particularly across the rim worlds.

## Monday Corporation (MDC)

Monday Corporation began as a modest software company and grew through centuries of acquisitions into a sprawling technology and services conglomerate. Today it operates across software, communications, finance, consumer technology, entertainment and infrastructure. Its famously unremarkable name has survived every merger and rebranding campaign, becoming one of the most recognizable corporate identities in human space.

## House of Roth (ROTH)

House of Roth traces its origins to a network of Euro banking families whose fortunes grew alongside the great diamond, oil and mineral empires of the twentieth and twenty-first centuries. Today the House controls extensive interests in finance, energy, mining, commodities and property, combining immense inherited wealth with generations of accumulated corporate influence. It remains one of the galaxy’s great old-money institutions.

## Seven Bells Inc (SBI)

Seven Bells began as a small family manufacturer of precision bells and signaling equipment, eventually expanding into communications, electronics and industrial systems. Today it is a diversified conglomerate with major interests in communications, manufacturing, consumer technology, aerospace and infrastructure. The corporation retains a peculiar affection for its origins, and seven bells remain a prominent symbol throughout its branding.

## General Industrial (GI)

GI is a conglomerate whose interests extend across manufacturing, energy, mining, construction, logistics and infrastructure. Its corporate philosophy is brutally simple: own the things everyone else needs. General Industrial has little public identity beyond its deliberately anonymous name, but its subsidiaries are embedded throughout the galactic economy, giving it extraordinary reach and leverage.

## Morrow & Sons (MNS)

Morrow & Sons began as a provider of funerary services. Its interests now extend into life insurance, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, property and cryogenic preservation. Its business begins where other corporations' responsibilities end, managing every stage of death and remembrance. Morrow maintains an immense portfolio of cemeteries, memorial habitats and long-term preservation facilities throughout human space.

## The Octagon (OCT)

The Octagon is an opaque financial conglomerate whose origins are deliberately obscure and whose interests extend far beyond conventional banking. It operates across investment, insurance, commodities, property and corporate finance, with a vast network of subsidiaries and holdings.

## The Meridian Company (TMC)

TMC is a vast diversified corporation with roots in global trade, shipping and navigation. Today its interests span transportation, finance, communications, energy, infrastructure and consumer services, with operations extending across much of human space. Meridian's reputation is one of relentless expansion, and its distinctive name has become synonymous with connectivity, movement and commerce.

## Crown & Anchor (CA)

Crown & Anchor is an old and enormously wealthy corporation with deep roots in shipping, insurance and maritime commerce. Today it operates across finance, transportation, property, hospitality and infrastructure, with particular strength in ports, orbital docks and long-distance logistics. Its conservative culture and distinctive old-world branding conceal a highly sophisticated and aggressively expanding commercial empire.

## Universal House (UNI)

Universal House is a sprawling consumer and commercial conglomerate with interests in retail, hospitality, food, entertainment, property and household goods. Its businesses range from enormous city malls and hotel complexes to everyday products found in homes across human space. Universal House has built its reputation on convenience, scale and an uncanny ability to make almost anything into a franchise.

## Evergreen Group (EG)

Evergreen Group is a diversified corporation built around agriculture, natural resources and environmental services. Its interests span food production, forestry, energy, water management, biotechnology and planetary development. Evergreen controls extensive tracts of productive land and has become a major provider of the resources required to sustain growing populations, giving it considerable influence over both established worlds and the frontier.

## Atlas Concern (ATL)

Atlas Concern is a vast industrial and infrastructure conglomerate with interests in mining, construction, energy, transportation and planetary development. It specializes in projects too large, remote or difficult for most corporations to undertake, from deep-space extraction to entire cities and orbital facilities. Its immense engineering capacity and appetite for expansion have made Atlas indispensable to the growth of human space.

## The Hanseatic Guild (HANS)

The Hanseatic Guild is an ancient commercial institution descended from the Euro merchant leagues. It operates across shipping, finance, trade, warehousing and insurance, maintaining an extensive network of ports, markets and trading houses throughout human space. Its members value long-term relationships and mutual obligation, and the Guild remains a formidable force in interplanetary commerce.

## Continuity Foundation (CON)

The Continuity Foundation has enterprises in education, healthcare, biotechnology, archives, infrastructure and long-term planetary development. It maintains enormous libraries, genetic repositories and cultural archives throughout human space, while its investment and development arms give it considerable influence over the future of entire worlds.

## Four Rivers Zaibatsu (FRZ)

Four Rivers Zaibatsu is an ancient Japanese conglomerate descended from one of the great industrial families of Old Earth. Its interests span banking, manufacturing, energy, shipping, technology and consumer goods, with a network of subsidiary companies bound together by generations of family ownership and corporate tradition. The Zaibatsu remains notoriously private, conservative and exceptionally difficult for outsiders to influence.

## Chettiar Holdings (CHET)

Chettiar Holdings traces its origins to the Indo merchant-banking communities of Old Earth. Today it is a diversified conglomerate with major interests in finance, commodities, property, shipping, manufacturing and infrastructure. Its extensive networking and reputation for careful stewardship have made Chettiar one of the galaxy’s most enduring commercial institutions.

## Andean Consolidated (AC)

AC is a vast resource and infrastructure conglomerate descended from the great Latino mining, agricultural and industrial enterprises. Its interests span mining, energy, agriculture, manufacturing, construction and transportation, with particular strength in planetary resource extraction. Andean's expertise in operating across difficult terrain has made it a major force in frontier development throughout human space.

## Dar al-Nour Trust (DAN)

Dar al-Nour Trust is an ancient institution descended from Islamic charitable endowments established on Old Earth. Its holdings span healthcare, education, finance, property, agriculture and infrastructure, with its wealth dedicated to maintaining institutions intended to endure indefinitely.

## Guangzhou Mercantile (GZ)

Guangzhou Mercantile traces its origins to the great Sino merchant houses, whose fortunes were built on maritime trade and long-distance commerce. Today it is a diversified corporation with major interests in shipping, finance, commodities, manufacturing, logistics and consumer goods. Its extensive trading network connects markets throughout human space, while its traditional merchant culture remains central to the company's identity.

## Mercury Communications (HG)

Mercury Communications is a dominant provider of communications infrastructure throughout human space. Its networks span planetary telecommunications, interplanetary relays, navigation systems, data services and broadcasting infrastructure, connecting corporations, habitats and billions of individuals. Mercury's immense network is effectively indispensable to modern commerce, and its reputation for reliability has made its distinctive name synonymous with communication itself.

## Orion Aerospace (ORA)

Orion Aerospace is one of the galaxy's dominant spacecraft manufacturers, producing everything from small private vessels to enormous commercial and industrial ships. Its interests extend into propulsion, avionics, shipyards, aerospace engineering and orbital infrastructure. Orion's ships are known for reliability and longevity, and its extensive network of yards and service facilities supports fleets throughout human space.

## Greenfields Corporation (GC)

Greenfields Corporation is an agricultural and food conglomerate, controlling extensive interests in farming, livestock, food processing, biotechnology, water management and distribution. Its operations range from vast automated plantations to food brands found throughout human space. Greenfields is particularly influential on populous worlds, where its supply networks feed billions of people every day.

## BioGenesis Life Sciences (BLS)

BioGenesis Life Sciences is a major pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation with interests spanning medicine, genetics, reproductive technology and advanced biological research. Its products range from everyday pharmaceuticals to sophisticated therapies and engineered biological systems. BioGenesis operates extensive research facilities throughout human space and maintains a particularly strong position in treatments designed to extend, enhance and restore human life.

## LiveWorlds (LW)

LiveWorlds is an entertainment conglomerate specializing in live experiences, interactive media, sports, gaming, virtual reality, social media, celebrity culture, and it operates a seldom-mentioned sideline in propaganda and industrial espionage. It operates stadiums, arenas, resorts, virtual worlds and entertainment networks throughout human space, while representing many of its most famous performers and athletes. Its business is built around turning audiences into participants, and experiences into enormous recurring revenues.

## Chimera Corporation (CHI)

Chimera began as an eccentric research enterprise and now owns an extraordinary portfolio of biotechnology, artificial intelligence, entertainment, pharmaceuticals and experimental technologies, with a sideline in theme parks, contracting and software. There is not always a clear distinction between its commercial and scientific ambitions.

## The Praetorium (PRAE)

The Praetorium is a provider of private security, legal services and arbitration, complemented by an extensive luxury division offering secure residences, travel, hospitality and personal services to the galaxy's most powerful clients.

## Terra Nova (TNI)

Terra Nova is a planetary resource extraction corporation specializing in mining, agriculture and environmental engineering. It operates mines, industrial plantations and genetically engineered food systems, alongside water extraction, forestry and terraforming. Terra Nova's philosophy is simple: a planet is a resource to be optimized, and its enormous appetite for land and materials makes it a dominant force in frontier development.

## Orion Spur Company (OSC)

OSC is an ancient trading corporation whose origins lie in the beginnings of commercial space travel. Over centuries it expanded into shipping, finance, resource extraction and general commerce, eventually becoming one of the largest diversified corporations in human space. The company is known for its relentless expansion and aggressive acquisition strategy, routinely entering markets where it has little previous presence and quickly establishing a challenging position.

## DeepSpace Cooperative (DSC)

DeepSpace Cooperative traces its origins to the first generation of humans born away from Earth. The early off-world communities pooled their resources to survive in isolated settlements, gradually developing a cooperative commercial structure that grew alongside the frontier. Today DSC is a vast diversified corporation owned collectively by its members, with interests spanning mining, shipping, manufacturing, finance and planetary development.
