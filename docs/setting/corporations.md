## Galactic Outcomes

Galactic Outcomes has its roots in the turbulent African politics of the late twentieth and early twenty-first century. The company began by supplying local mercenaries to the highest bidder, a lucrative market at the time. Today they are by far the largest supplier of all forms of hired muscle from bouncers and bodyguards to soldiers and assassins. They include a profitable subdivision that manufactures all forms of small arms and ammunition for both the personal and corporate market, and another that specializes in non-combat personnel like scientists, engineers and accountants.

## Karaquazen Inc

Karaquazen is a corporation respected for its monopoly as much as it is feared for its methods. It runs a galactic chain of mega-casinos and gambling houses, and operates 95% of all the planetary lotteries. Shady dealings are second nature to Karaquazen, and it has never been totally constrained by the law. Although it is not widely known, Karaquazen also has a controlling interest in nearly all medical aid and insurance providers.

## Hamatomo Triad

While some corporations may linger on the borders of the law, the Hamatomo Triad is downright illegal. Operating from the rim worlds, the triad has built a monopoly on illegal drugs of all kinds, and is partial to other forms of crime from extortion and money laundering to assassination. It is the rival of any corporation in wealth and influence, and considerably more bloodthirsty than most. Most of the other corporations have had dealings with the triad at one time or another, and it is rumoured that the blackmail potential may make the Hamatomo Triad even more powerful than mere figures would suggest.

The triad traces origins to **Tibetan triads** that rose in the late twenty-first century.

## MicroDonald Franchise

Born of the merger between several Americano-descended companies, MicroDonald specialises in low cost, high volume consumer goods and services of all kinds. Behind its high-powered advertising campaign and cute mascot, MDF is a cunning and ruthless monopoly that has annihilated all competition. It is possible, and increasingly common, for a person to use exclusively MDF products from the moment he is delivered in a city-mall hospital to the day he is buried in an MDF coffin.

## Disney Worlds

Once a benign entertainment company, Disney Worlds has learned to exploit the mass media on a galactic scale. They hold the monopoly on HV broadcasts and channel content, and are the acknowledged experts of advertising. Their true money source, however, is their Corporate Liaisons wing — which specialises in all forms of propaganda and espionage.

## GRC

GRC is the youngest of the large corporations, and it is in direct competition with the older and larger Creus Corp. Both corporations deal in the investment banking and speculation market. GRC sprang into being a mere two generations ago when an uncharacteristic slip by Creus left the market wide open for a competitor. The speed with which GRC established itself has led to rumours that it is merely a front for another corporation, but GRC's energetic second generation CEO has repeatedly denied this.

## Creus Corporation

Creus is an old and respected investment bank and one of the richest corporations out there, at least in terms of declared assets. Their banking operations are under threat from the upstart GRC, but they still hold the monopoly when it comes to auction houses, micro loans and property markets.

## Futtbuckers

Regarded as somewhat of a minnow by the other corporations, Futtbuckers is nonetheless wealthy and powerful enough to be mentioned with the best of them. It operates a number of different restaurant franchises, most notably its chain of up market coffee shops. Futtbuckers is involved in the entire supply chain of these franchises, right back to third world based agriculture on isolated rim worlds. Many analysts speculate that Futtbuckers has significant additional sources of income that are as yet unknown.

## Holt-Winters Corp

Holt-Winters is a relatively young corporation that has a reputation for energy and innovation. They have the major market share of shield generators and compact power sources, and market several light space ships — including the **Flare-ON** line featured in the prototype player ship.

## Spiotvolle und Schtüllein (SuS)

Originally born out of the ashes of the European Union Industry Conglomerate, SuS is the high tech manufacturer of weapons and weapon accessories. Their major business is in building the variety of heavy weapons available for fitting on space ships, but they also produce a variety of high end personal, vehicle and planetary armaments. They have a reputation for high quality and standards, and many pilots swear by their SuS armaments.

## Durbin-Watson Inc

DW is another of the younger corporations. It produces a range of products from some space ship configurations through various seemingly random ship components down to a strange array of consumer electronics. DW is not often in the news, and analysts speculate that their strange product line is part of a complex and secret strategy. In a galaxy of monopolies, DW is a jack-of-all-trades — best known for the **Krypton** mid-range saucer ships.

## Bayes Inc

Bayes is an old and respected engineering firm. They primarily manufacture armour, engines and chassis for a variety of space ships and vehicles. They have several large and profitable subdivisions, including one that specialises in life support systems and another that is the largest research and development wing of any of the corporations. Prototype JSON uses Bayes as maker for Mark 1 / Mark 3 fusion engines and 5mm Chitanium armour.

## Tukey Enterprises

Tukey Enterprises produces only space ship components. Their strategy seems to be to fill the gaps in the market left by the other corporations, from armour and shields through light engines and weapons to certain light space ship configurations. Their diversified approach seems to have worked for them, but rumours are growing that the corporation is in serious financial trouble.

## SnedeCorp

Software is the game of SnedeCorp. It has over time amalgamated thousands of different software companies to gain the monopoly in the market. They manufacture all kinds of software from games to commercial software to the complex systems found on modern space ships. SnedeCorp has a reputation as a paranoid and secretive company.

## Kolmogorov-Smirnov

KS was born out of the union of the military-industries of the former Russian Empire. It specialises in heavy weapons and heavier space ship configurations. The largest planetary defense platforms, heaviest attack ships and most powerful sub-nuclear personal arms come from this corporation — including the **Dragon** interceptor family.

## Oklahoma Combine

Makes the **Silhouette** medium-weight tactical fighter; Mk8 configuration most popular in catalog lore.
